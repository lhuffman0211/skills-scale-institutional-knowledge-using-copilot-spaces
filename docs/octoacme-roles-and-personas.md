# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers
## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure project work meets release readiness requirements before shipping to customers.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality gates for each milestone or release
- Create and maintain test plans, scenarios, and acceptance criteria checks
- Execute manual and automated testing for new features and regressions
- Identify, triage, and track defects with engineering and product teams
- Partner with Developers to improve testability, reliability, and CI coverage
- Validate that deliverables meet Definition of Done before release

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable features
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Improve feedback loops for quality issues
- Ensure stakeholder confidence in release quality

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and release readiness reviews
- Daily standups for testing blockers and risk visibility
- PR and design review feedback on testability and edge cases
- QA signoff for major milestones and releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate code quality, catch regressions, and improve automation
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and customer-impacting scenarios
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestone readiness, risk tracking, and delivery gates
- Supports stakeholders by providing evidence of quality before launch

---

## Technical Architect / Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical direction, ensure systems are scalable and maintainable, and help the team make sound engineering decisions that align with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements for technical feasibility and complexity
- Define architecture standards, patterns, and integration approaches
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and long-term trade-offs
- Guide design decisions and technical direction across the project
- Mentor Developers and support solution design quality
- Ensure systems remain secure, scalable, and aligned with organizational standards

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable and resilient solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architecture drift
- Improve decision quality for complex implementation choices
- Enable delivery teams to scale confidently as requirements grow

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Planning sessions for scope, dependencies, and sequencing
- Technical risk review with Project Managers and leadership
- Design and code review feedback with engineering teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to shape implementation decisions and technical quality
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery constraints
- Works with Project Managers to identify dependencies, risks, and cross-team impacts
- Provides governance for technical standards that support stakeholder confidence and operational stability

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Sponsors or stakeholder representatives provide business direction, funding support, and executive alignment so the project remains connected to strategic goals and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project scope, priorities, and major decision gates
- Provide business context, constraints, and strategic direction
- Escalate risks and unresolved dependencies impacting delivery
- Support resource allocation and stakeholder alignment
- Review progress against business value and success metrics
- Make go/no-go decisions at milestone checkpoints

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Keep the initiative aligned with strategic priorities
- Support timely decision-making and stakeholder alignment
- Reduce uncertainty and escalation delays

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and milestone reviews
- Executive updates and status briefings
- Escalation channels for major blockers or decision needs
- Sponsor approvals and release/governance checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on priorities, business outcomes, and success metrics
- Works with Project Managers to monitor delivery health and escalate issues
- Provides strategic input for roadmap decisions and trade-offs
- Supports Developers and engineering by clarifying business context and constraints

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches facilitate delivery practices, remove impediments, and help teams improve collaboration, flow, and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help teams identify and resolve blockers or process friction
- Coach teams on agile practices, ownership, and collaboration norms
- Support continuous improvement and learning across delivery cycles
- Track team health, process issues, and opportunities for improvement
- Improve cross-team coordination when dependencies or conflicts arise

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery rhythm
- Reduce friction, confusion, and wasted effort
- Strengthen psychological safety and continuous learning
- Support predictable and sustainable execution

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and team rituals
- Retrospectives and process improvement discussions
- Dependency and conflict resolution with cross-functional stakeholders
- Feedback to Project Managers and Product Managers on team health and delivery impediments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers by removing blockers and improving team practices
- Helps Product Managers and Project Managers align scope, planning, and priorities with team capacity
- Encourages stakeholder transparency through effective team communication and ceremony discipline
- Works with leadership to identify patterns that affect delivery quality and throughput

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure the project meets policy, regulatory, and security requirements throughout planning, development, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for the project
- Review architectural and implementation choices for risk exposure
- Support threat modeling, secure design reviews, and security testing
- Ensure compliance with relevant controls, policies, and legal obligations
- Partner with teams during release planning to verify readiness and controls
- Support incident response and post-incident remediation planning

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Enable secure-by-design development
- Protect customer trust and organizational reputation
- Align delivery work with approved standards and controls

### Typical Communication
- Planning and design reviews
- Security signoff checkpoints and compliance reviews
- Release readiness checks and risk escalation paths
- Incident coordination and remediation follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure coding practices and risk mitigation
- Works with Product Managers on risk trade-offs, compliance needs, and stakeholder expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release gates, dependencies, and escalation paths
- Helps Sponsors and stakeholders understand risk implications and governance requirements

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
